Privacy and data access

Admin can access all records. Faculty can access students in their assigned department. Student accounts can access only their linked student record.

The database stores accounts with hashed passwords, educational indicators, support actions and an activity log. Session cookies are required for sign-in and request protection. No student data is sent to an external AI provider.

Included DEMO records are synthetic. Before using real student data, your institution must define its own retention, consent and access policies.
