Privacy policy

Applies to the Smart Campus Analytics platform.

Who can see data

This version opens without login. Anyone who can reach the app can access student data and all Admin controls, including account management.

What is stored

  • Staff accounts: username, display name, role and a salted password hash. Passwords are never stored in readable form.
  • Student records: roll number, department, year, and indicators for academics, attendance, LMS activity, engagement, placement, skills and feedback.
  • An audit log of data changes, account changes and exports, recorded as local-admin with the time. This shared label does not identify individual visitors.

Cookies

One strictly necessary session cookie protects against forged requests. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and no third-party services.

How student data is used

To help authorised faculty and administrators identify students who may need academic or placement support. Scores and risk flags are decision support for people, not automatic judgements about a student.

Retention and your rights

Records are kept only as long as the institution needs them and are handled under applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. To ask about, correct or erase a record, contact the administrator of your institution.

Demo data

Records with IDs beginning DEMO- are synthetic and do not describe real people.

Contact

Contact the project team through the CSE department at St Mary's Engineering College.
